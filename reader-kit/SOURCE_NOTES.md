# Source notes

This public source kit adapts a four-reference research guide and local reader prototype authored for the 2026-10-21 application pilot. The source guide, BibTeX entries, source summaries, HTML template and browser script are bundled as editable files.

The summaries preserve the authored claim wording and review states; they are citation aids, not independent validation. In particular, the Walton entry remains held pending review of the erratum. The guide presents BKI and IN2 as HUMMBL design interpretations, not evidence that the framework or reader is effective.

The source bibliography contains citation metadata and links only. It does not include papers, abstracts, private corpus data, evaluation tasks, participant information, internal proposals, governance records or session material. Source links are external URLs and are never fetched by the build.

The reader source was prepared on 2026-10-21. This public source snapshot is derived from the then-current prototype; its bundle identity will differ from the private prototype because this package has a new, reduced provenance set.
