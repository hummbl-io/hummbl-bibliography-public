01 / Question

Start with something you need to make.

A design decision, research note, evaluation plan, or paragraph you could explain to a colleague. Write your first answer before asking an assistant to improve it.

02 / Evidence

Compare the finding with your context.

These studies examine different settings and interventions. They do not form a universal verdict on AI or belonging, and they have not tested this reader kit. Open a source to inspect the supporting section.

A DOI identifies a work; it does not verify a claim. Reading-path references are citation candidates, not claimed members of the frozen public bibliography snapshot.

03 / Decision

Show the steps in your judgment.

Keep the source finding, your interpretation and your proposed action separate. An assistant can challenge an inference or offer a hint; you still choose what belongs in your work.

Context, reasoning lens and review notes

A reasoning lens is optional. HUMMBL's IN2: Premortem Analysis asks you to assume failure and work backward to its causes. It can help identify what a polished answer might conceal; it does not validate the source.

04 / Keep it

Take the work with you.

Export a readable decision record, an editable JSON copy, or the four references. An export is a saved draft, not a quality score or independent review.
