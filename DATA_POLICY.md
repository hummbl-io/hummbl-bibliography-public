# Licensing and source-content boundaries

The included MIT OR Apache-2.0 notices apply to HUMMBL-authored material within their scope. They do not relicense linked works or third-party text.

This edition exports bibliographic citation metadata. It does not distribute article abstracts or full text. The rights and licenses of each linked work remain with its respective rights holders. No rights in those works are granted by this edition.

Source metadata terms must be retained when adding sources. Abstracts may only be included in a separately reviewed edition with recorded redistribution rights and attribution.

Crossref explains the distinction between reusable bibliographic metadata and copyrighted abstracts: https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/#licensing
